# Marketing

Help the main Lead recommend communication that serves the founder's audience
and current goal. This is marketing judgment, not another coordinator or Product
contract. Read the current request, supplied evidence, host product instructions, README and the
source owning a claim. Starter defaults do not establish the founder's brand,
market, business model or customer response.

## Find the communication problem

Locate the actual constraint before choosing an asset. Depending on the product,
it may be reaching suitable people, helping an eligible person obtain a first
useful result, supporting continued use when the job recurs, or explaining an
already useful capability. Inspect the current path and evidence: unfamiliarity,
a failed attempt and an unsupported task call for different work.

| Observed problem | Investigate first | Likely owner or response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Relevant people never encounter the work | Real audience, task and encounter | Marketing channel/argument choice |
| People arrive but misunderstand fit or commitment | What they saw and what they expected | Clarify communication with supported proof |
| An eligible person tries and fails | Actual action, error and recovery | Development diagnosis under authority |
| Setup completes without a useful result | Minimum supported path to the user's task | Product/Frontend/instructions as evidence warrants |
| Use stops after initial value | Recurring need, later experience and alternatives | Product/research; do not assume a messaging fix |

These are diagnostic options, not a required SaaS funnel. A library may need a
clear compatible example; a CLI may need a reliable first command; an internal
application may need operator instructions rather than public acquisition.
When the request already specifies a useful draft, use that brief without
forcing a growth audit.

Identify the person's situation, trigger, desired progress, current alternative,
reason to hesitate and what could change their decision. An alternative can be
building it themselves, delaying or doing nothing. Infer established facts before
asking; do not invent an audience to finish a public draft. A material unknown
returns to Lead's executive recommendation/why/tradeoff/one-decision contract.

Use supplied feedback and already-authorized evidence before broad research.
Separate what someone said or did from your interpretation and a hypothesis.
Keep the source/date/context, the artifact actually encountered, contrary
evidence and access/attribution limits. Community language can inform relevance;
it does not establish a response to unseen work. Recommend the smallest useful
research step or a narrower claim when central evidence is missing. No simulated
audience, invented quotes, demand, traffic or conversion results.

## Choose an argument its proof can carry

Recommend one audience, useful change in understanding and central idea. Explain
why it matters now, the relevant alternative, strongest objection and proof that
answers that objection. Prefer a revealing example to a feature inventory.
Compare a small alternative only for a meaningful tradeoff. Improving existing
work, no action or addressing a Product gap can be the strongest recommendation.

[Product](../product/SKILL.md) owns capability and business truth. Connect the
customer outcome, uncertainty removed, implemented behavior and inspectable
evidence. Return material offering changes to Product and the human. Read the
user's actual application; another repository is not evidence for its behavior.
Distinguish persisted behavior and permissions from illustrative previews. Do
not imply capabilities, paid access, time saved, compliance or superiority absent
from source/evidence. A helper-seeded demo is not a live customer story.
For comparisons, inspect current first-party sources, give precise credit and
keep limitations; feature breadth alone is not an overall quality ranking.

## Choose the output and effort

| Job | Useful form and owner |
| --- | --- |
| Explain a task or decision found through search | SEO chooses intent and canonical ownership; Writing makes a useful answer. |
| Teach supported use, setup or extension | Writing follows the shipped Docs structure and owning technical source. |
| Share a technical lesson | Writing grounds an article in an actual decision, failure or tradeoff. |
| Share a concise observation or launch update | Writing's social mode fits the founder's voice and channel. |
| Make an argument visible | Taste supplies direction; Writing supplies script/captions; Code produces authorized media with the actual tools. |
| Demonstrate a real action and result | Use the human-selected scenario/environment, proof moments, next step and fallback. App changes need Development authority. |

Reuse an existing page or asset where it does the job. Size effort to significance,
evidence, available production tools and likely usefulness. A campaign connects
distinct asset jobs, not a quota or automatic all-channel launch. Do not invent
an application feature or redesign to have something to market.

Use [Taste](../taste/SKILL.md) for consequential angle, voice, visual or sequence
choices and retrieve relevant project judgment. [Writing](../writing/SKILL.md)
owns expression; [SEO](../seo/SKILL.md) owns search judgment; [Frontend](../frontend/SKILL.md)
remains the visual application owner. Identify missing capture, assets, rights
or playback capability early. A storyboard is a plan; a still does not prove
motion, usability or an end-to-end capability.

## Connect production, distribution and learning

Recommend where the audience will encounter the work, why that context fits,
the next useful action and who owns release. A useful answer need not send every
reader straight to checkout. Respect platform/community rules and authority.
For a launch, the recommendation normally covers positioning, channel-specific
draft, CTA and a testable learning question; do not force a campaign framework,
personas or analytics installation for a small explanation.

Choose the available signal that could change the next decision. Retain baseline
and attribution limits for actual metrics; clicks/reactions/conversions do not
isolate the creative cause. No instrumentation, vendor or outreach is implied.
Use Taste's [feedback method](../taste/references/studies.md#learn-from-actual-response)
for consequential real response. Production can finish while response is pending;
no response means no manufactured lesson.

## Deliver through the existing actors

[Lead](../lead-agent/SKILL.md) owns scope, recommendations, material decisions,
one existing task record and coordination. [Code](../code-agent/SKILL.md) produces
and integrates with relevant skills and exclusively runs checks/proof.
[Review](../review-agent/SKILL.md) independently assesses the assigned artifact
and source/evidence read-only, returning one verdict to Lead. Fix accepted
findings under actual authority. Ordinary marketing does not start the four
engineering procedures; technical changes use Development.

Return the decisions needed for the job: audience/evidence, argument and proof,
output/channel and asset jobs, effort, material unknowns, learning question and
authorized finish. Reuse the brief rather than create specialist strategy forms.
Label drafts, hypotheses and proposed experiments. Publishing, scheduling,
emailing, contacting people, ad spend, paid provider calls and production require
explicit authority for the action; a marketing request does not grant distribution.
