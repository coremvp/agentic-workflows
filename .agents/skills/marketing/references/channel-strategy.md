# Channel strategy

Load when audience encounter or platform choice materially changes the work.
Begin with where relevant people already solve/discuss the task, their state of
awareness and what an artifact can usefully do there.

| Encounter | Fits when | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search / owned answer | People have a specific question | Canonical ownership, supported answer and discoverability |
| Community reply | A live discussion needs relevant help | Rules, relevance, trust and unsolicited promotion |
| Founder social post | Existing context/voice can carry a lesson | Missing proof, context collapse, unsuitable audience |
| Professional feed | Work situation and credible experience matter | Generic corporate copy and reach mistaken for demand |
| Visual/video feed | The mechanism benefits from seeing it | Capture/playback constraints and weak unsupported spectacle |
| Email / owned release channel | Audience expects that communication | Consent, eligibility, actual distribution authority |

These are encounter choices, not a prescription to use every platform. Existing
access and resources matter; don't create accounts to make a plan appear complete.
Match format to evidence and next action. A complex technical tradeoff might
need an owned explanation with a useful social excerpt; a reproducible interaction
might benefit from a captured demonstration.

## Decide and bound

Recommend a primary context, why the audience/asset fits, distinctive job,
requested format, next action and important limitation. A small alternative is
useful only when the tradeoff is real. Use [social media specs](social-media-specs.md)
for current applicable constraints and [X algorithm](x-algorithm.md) only when
X mechanisms matter. Platform features/rules should be verified against current
primary sources rather than recalled as permanent limits.

Writing owns the actual words/medium; [content promotion](content-promotion.md)
owns connected encounters when needed. Maintain proof and founder voice across
adaptation. No fixed calendar, engagement ratio, “best” posting hour or automatic
all-channel package follows from choosing a channel. Results need real exposure,
response and attribution context; platform fit is a reasoned hypothesis.
