# A/B testing

Load when a controlled comparison could resolve a consequential uncertainty.
Testing is one evidence method, not the mandatory finish for conversion work.

## Decide whether to test

Define the decision, eligible population, outcome frequency, effect worth acting
on, available time and measurement integrity. A statistician/appropriate method
may be needed for power or complex designs; don't invent sample requirements or
significance from a traffic count. Low traffic, rare outcomes, changing cohorts,
missing allocation or a product failure can make a test uninformative.

For an underpowered situation, recommend justified deferral or proportionate
alternatives: inspect task completion, repair a reproducible error, interview
eligible users, or validate the measurement. State what evidence/volume/stability
would make a controlled comparison useful. A valid non-test answer needs no
fictional control or target p-value.

## Design a feasible comparison

Specify one hypothesis linking observed friction to a change and useful outcome.
Define current control, treatment, eligible unit, consistent assignment, exposure
and durable outcome. Avoid switching versions mid-session or counting repeat
visits as independent people. Choose guardrails for qualification, error rates,
privacy and harmful behavior where relevant.

| Observed uncertainty | Candidate change | What would support it / alternative explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Users misunderstand commitment | Explain actual cost/next step beside action | Comprehension + qualified completion; traffic mix can confound |
| Optional detail blocks progress | Defer nonessential field | Completion without lost qualification; dependency may require it |
| Readers miss evidence | Move supported proof nearer objection | Relevant action; novelty/layout changes can confound |
| Wrong audience attempts | Clarify eligibility | Fewer unsuitable attempts, not necessarily more total clicks |

Name duration/stop rules, analysis plan, acceptable effect and uncertainty before
seeing results, using actual available statistical support. Account for full
business cycles, uneven allocation, overlapping experiments, device/source mix
and novelty. Repeated peeking or stopping at a favorable reading can mislead;
choose an appropriate planned method rather than invent certainty.

## Interpret before rolling out

Check assignment/measurement validity and guardrails before comparing outcomes.
Report counts, time window, method, uncertainty and practical tradeoff. An
inconclusive result is not a win; a smaller qualified audience can be preferable
to more low-value clicks. Investigate conflicts rather than select the prettiest
metric. No guaranteed lift or causal claim beyond the design's scope.

Return a recommendation and experiment plan or justified non-test path.
Instrumentation, allocation, app changes and rollout require Development/Code
and explicit authority; loading this guide never starts an experiment.
