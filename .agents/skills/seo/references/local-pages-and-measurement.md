# Local pages and measurement

Load when a real service/location needs a useful page or local measurement
interpretation. Product facts and actual customer questions determine page value.

## Earn each page

Name the distinct reader task, geography/service actually covered, relevant
information and existing canonical owner. Useful differences may include real
availability, access, service conditions, directions or local constraints.
Changing a city name in identical paragraphs doesn't create a distinct answer.
Prefer one honest service-area page to thin invented branches.

For a fictional repair business serving two areas with the same process, one
page can explain coverage. Separate pages may earn their place if access/services
materially differ and the business can substantiate them. An online-only app can
provide a regional policy answer without pretending it has a local office.

Keep name/address/service/eligibility consistent with actual source and provider
records. Markup should describe visible truthful facts. Valid structured data is
not proof of rich-result eligibility, profile eligibility, indexing or rank;
check the applicable current official feature guidance before claiming any.

## Measure the appropriate stage

Record source, baseline period, geography/page/query, denominator and limits.
Separate visibility/impressions, clicks, qualified inquiries, completed work and
revenue. Compare compatible windows/cohorts; seasonality, market changes and
multiple edits can confound attribution. A local ranking screenshot isn't a
causal business result.

Recommend a meaningful outcome signal using existing authorized evidence. Don't
install analytics or track sensitive user data by default. Missing data stays
missing. Use [profiles and citations](local-profiles-and-citations.md) for identity/
provider constraints and [search opportunities](search-opportunities.md) for
canonical/intent prioritization.

Deliver page job/content corrections, source facts, technical owner/check need
and interpretation limits. Writing produces the answer, Development/Code execute
authorized markup/routes, and Review reads evidence. No automatic city-page
factory, review campaign or ranking/revenue promise.
