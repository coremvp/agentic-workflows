# Local SEO

Load when real local/service-area discovery matters. First decide whether this
business actually serves people locally and what useful question a local result
would answer. An online-only app isn't made local by buying an address.

Separate Maps/profile eligibility from organic page usefulness. A business may
have a useful regional explanation without qualifying for a provider profile.
Product/source truth establishes service model, locations, eligibility and actual
offer. Don't invent branches, reviews, credentials or customer response.

## Select the relevant path

- [Profiles and citations](local-profiles-and-citations.md): actual business model,
  eligibility, field/privacy decisions, duplicates and consistency.
- [Pages and measurement](local-pages-and-measurement.md): useful location/service
  answers, markup and evidence of discovery versus business outcomes.

Use current first-party platform guidance for eligibility/field rules and retain
source/date. Available data may be report excerpts, provided page facts or an
authorized observation; don't silently access accounts to fill gaps.

## Bound the recommendation

Name real customer geography, task/intent, current owner/profile/page, evidence,
useful correction and limitations. For a fictional online-only review app selling
worldwide, recommend useful organic answers where supported; don't set up a Maps
profile in a rented mailbox. A real in-person service business may need a
different recommendation.

Prioritize actual inconsistencies or missing useful information. A list of city
keywords isn't a page plan, and search visibility isn't attributed revenue.
Account/profile changes, outreach/review requests, technical implementation and
publication have their own authority. No automatic local campaign or tracker.
