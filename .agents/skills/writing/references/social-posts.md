# Social posts

Load for a post, thread or founder update. Use the actual audience, channel,
source claim and chosen voice, not an interchangeable engagement formula.

## Pick one supported idea

Useful openings include a recognizable failure, concrete shipped change, supported
observation, decision/tradeoff or question the body actually answers. Give the
reader a reason to care immediately. A provocative claim needs evidence; “nobody
is talking about this” is not earned merely because the topic is new to us.

| Structure | Original example direction | What the body owes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Failure → mechanism → lesson | Wrong revision reviewed; bind context to version | Actual causal distinction and limits |
| Shipped change → use → eligibility | Recover safe input after validation failure | Available behavior, not promised perfection |
| Tradeoff → decision → evidence | Keep file-based content for a small editing team | Actual constraints; no universal CMS verdict |
| Question → useful answer | When should a short update become a thread? | Distinct evidence/ideas worth the extra reading |

A thread should advance the argument with each post. Opening inventory/counts
must match delivered coverage; don't split one sentence across posts just to
create length. Links should lead to the relevant verified owner; placeholders
remain visibly placeholders.

## Preserve founder character and truth

Use applicable Taste without importing a permanent house voice. Technical
specificity and humor can coexist; don't flatten a useful observation into
corporate praise. Keep beta/local/fixture status when material. No invented
results, testimonials, screenshots, reach claims or engagement bait.

Check applicable [platform constraints](../../marketing/references/social-media-specs.md)
through current evidence; requested amount and channel matter. Recommend one
strong draft for an unspecified small job rather than generate automatic variants.
Return the selected draft and consequential assumptions. Posting/scheduling/
outreach/spending remain separately authorized.
