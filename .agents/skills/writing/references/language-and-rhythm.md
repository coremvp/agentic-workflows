# Language and rhythm

Load for a specific wording, transition, register or pacing problem. Language
choices depend on audience and meaning, not a universal list of forbidden words,
punctuation or supposed AI tells.

## Replace vagueness with the missing fact

| Weak in this context | More precise original example | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leverage our robust solution | Keep comments attached to the reviewed version | Names the behavior |
| Streamline onboarding | Resume setup after correcting the email field | Names a supported improvement |
| It is important to note | Invitations expire after the configured period | States the needed fact directly |
| This changes everything | The old link can no longer approve a newer revision | Gives the consequence |
| Users love it | Remove, or cite the actual response/context | Affection cannot be inferred from a feature |

Do not swap words while leaving the same empty thought. “Use” is often clearer
than “utilize,” but a technical term can be necessary. Preserve actual domain
names, uncertainty and the founder's useful voice. A deliberately formal legal
notice and an informal engineering story need different registers.

## Connect ideas through their relationship

Use cause (“Because the link is revision-bound…”), constraint (“When the user is
signed out…”), consequence (“That preserves…”), evidence (“The supplied trace
shows…”) or contrast when it helps the reader. Avoid adding a transition to every
paragraph. Place a caveat near the claim it qualifies rather than burying it later.

Attribution distinguishes observation and inference: “The excerpt reports…”;
“That suggests…”; “We still don't know…”. Avoid both overconfidence and hedge
stacks that hide a supported conclusion.

## Control pace without a sentence quota

One paragraph should usually develop one idea. A long sentence can carry a tight
relationship; split it when unrelated qualifications obscure the action. Vary
length with purpose. Read dense passages aloud or in their rendered context when
possible. Lists suit parallel choices/steps, not every thought. Preserve a sharp
short sentence when it earns emphasis; don't manufacture rhythmic fragments.

Delete filler, doubled qualifications and repeated labels. Check pronoun referents
and concrete verbs. Punctuation is a meaning tool; an em dash doesn't establish
authorship or poor quality. Return to [editing](editing.md) to judge whether the
whole revision still serves the reader rather than merely sounding tidy.
