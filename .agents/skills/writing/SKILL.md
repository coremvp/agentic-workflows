# Writing

Write for the founder's product and reader. Use current request, host product instructions,
README and the owning source to establish names, audience, behavior and commands.
If the brand is still a starter default, ask through Lead only when a public
draft needs the actual name; technical docs can use the feature name.

## Move from the brief to a useful draft

First choose the work: a new artifact needs a brief and argument; a draft with
useful voice needs diagnosis and revision; a point edit needs a bounded correction.
Preserve the accepted reader, intent and strong material instead of restarting
every assignment. Infer the brief from existing sources; return only material
missing decisions through Lead.

For new or structurally weak work, establish the reader's starting knowledge,
encounter, question, central supported claim and next useful action. Turn that
into a reader promise: what will they understand or be able to do after reading?
Choose a thesis the evidence can carry. Sketch sections by their distinct jobs,
placing the necessary claim, example, source or action under each. A heading
list without that evidence is not yet a useful outline.

Draft through the argument, then revise the consequential mismatch. Deliver
value early: an answer, recognizable consequence, concrete example or useful
inventory when it helps readers choose and apply the material. Put implementation
details where they help extension or troubleshooting. Each section should add
understanding or ability, rather than restate the introduction. A page has one
job; improve the nearest useful owner before adding another.

Check truth and argument before sentence polish; reread the whole changed
passage afterward so a local improvement does not remove a prerequisite,
exception or useful character. [Editing](references/editing.md) selects the
necessary passes. Read [content types](references/content-types.md) only for the
selected Docs, Help, article, social or script mode.

Distinguish included code, configured provider behavior, local/helper-seeded proof
and unverified claims. Persisted behavior and permissions differ from illustrative previews.
Do not claim capabilities, time savings, compliance, superiority or paid access
absent from source/evidence. Screenshots
support presentation, not permission/payment claims. Never expose secrets or
proof account credentials.

Use direct prose, specific nouns and a revealing concrete example. Delete filler,
unearned adjectives, fake metrics, interchangeable AI copy and decorative callouts.
Keep voice and character when clarifying; do not flatten every subject into the
same explanation. [Marketing](../marketing/SKILL.md) chooses audience/argument;
[Taste](../taste/SKILL.md) helps consequential angle, structure, rhythm or voice;
[SEO](../seo/SKILL.md) supplies search intent and canonical ownership when relevant.
Routine factual or mechanical edits do not need a Taste exercise.

[Code](../code-agent/SKILL.md) produces and integrates authorized prose/media and
alone runs applicable MDX/type/build, command or browser checks. Follow the literal
guide against real source; label what was not run. [Review](../review-agent/SKILL.md)
independently reads the assigned brief/artifact/source and evidence, returning
one publish/revise/block quality verdict and specific findings to Lead. That
verdict does not authorize publication. Fix accepted findings under actual authority.

Deliver local drafts unless the assigned finish explicitly authorizes repository
publication or distribution. Posting, scheduling, contacting people, spending,
provider/production access and paid calls keep their own permission boundaries.
Use Taste's feedback method only for actual consequential response; no invented
audience reaction or learning to fill an empty record.

## Keep substantive claims within their source

Check factual meaning in every substantive claim, including small qualifiers in
recommendations, plans, summaries and internal handoffs, not only public headlines.
Keep the actor and relationship the source establishes; do not infer who someone
is to another person. A positive permission does not establish an exclusive one.
An object/link association does not prove someone viewed it, completed an action
or approved it. A field's presence does not establish all its allowed values or
that it is the only deciding condition.

When a qualifier exceeds the evidence, narrow/remove it, mark the inference as
uncertain or ask for the source if the decision depends on it. Domain owners
remain authoritative for capability, authorization and schema truth. Preserve
harmless metaphor, voice and clearly hypothetical examples; this is a factual
check, not a demand for sterile prose or a claim table for every sentence.
