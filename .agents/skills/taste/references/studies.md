# Reference studies and real feedback

Read for close study, critique or a consequential actual response. Select an
exact work and question; do not load a canon or imitate a house style.

## A study that changes a choice

Experience the work before commentary and record a first response. Annotate what
is present separately from inference: region for an image, timestamp for a
sequence, passage for prose. Compare a related work, identify a consequential
difference and where the preferred device fails. Make a small original study,
predict its effect, inspect it and seek the kind of response the uncertainty
needs. Revisit the judgment on another example; preserve changed minds and
exceptions rather than aesthetic commandments.

For critique, state intention/uncertainty, invite specific response and ask neutral
questions before defending the work. Practitioner methods and admired references
are inputs to inspect, not scientific rankings or evidence of this audience's
reaction. If following a named method, read its actual steps rather than present
this loose loop as that method.

## Learn from actual response

Use the existing task record, not a new learning workflow or interview form.
Retain consequential details: artifact/version or exact passage/frame, intended
effect, audience/context, source/date, what was said/done, interpretation, decision
affected and limitation. Preserve enough observation to distinguish it from the
explanation; inspect the supplied artifact when the interpretation depends on it.

A human's explicit preference is authoritative within its scope. An actual user
response establishes that observed experience, not an invented segment or a
universal effect. Community/support language can inform relevance without testing
unseen work. Practitioner critique and AI interpretations are readings, not
customer evidence. Metrics retain baseline and attribution limits; changing
several variables does not isolate cause.

Compare response with artifact and intention. Diagnose relevance, comprehension,
credibility, voice, composition, interaction or craft. Revise a consequential
choice inside authorization; a material new direction returns to the human.
Code supplies needed capture/render/proof. Distinguish prediction, inspected
result and actual later response.

When a lesson could change later work, Lead curates it in
[project judgment](project-judgment.md): when useful, judgment kind, recoverable
source/observation, decision informed, scope/exception and revision trigger.
Code persists Lead-curated canonical support/instruction changes under actual
continuing authorization; Review consumes them read-only. Saving an entry edits
that tracked file. When current human authority does not cover the write, Lead
proposes the curated entry with recommendation, reason and tradeoff and records
it as pending in the existing task record. Pending is a proposed lesson, not a
saved project preference; continue the authorized artifact work without treating
silence or elapsed time as acceptance.
Keep a self-contained non-identifying summary; private scratch locators may supply
provenance but cannot be the whole explanation. No raw private conversations,
customer/account data, secrets, payloads or large copied artifacts in the skill.
Use supplied/already-authorized evidence; collection, outreach, account access
and paid calls require their own authority.

No arbitrary response count is required for a narrow lesson. Interpretations
without an observed audience effect stay hypotheses. Do not retain one-off
coordinates or punctuation. Consolidate duplicates; binding rules live in their
nearest domain owner with a reference here.

Before relevant later work, retrieve the entry and evidence, check the actual
context, exceptions and latest accepted direction, then explain the choice it
informs. If evidence is unavailable, preserve the distinction between an explicit
human preference and an unverified interpretation; do not invent the missing
rationale. Current human direction, Product truth and the medium owner prevail.
Contrary evidence narrows, revises or removes the lesson; keep useful changed-mind
reasoning in existing
task history. Missing evidence is unknown, not negative feedback. Production
may finish while response is pending; revisit only when a later authorized task
supplies actual evidence. No score, counter, status machine or required experiment.
