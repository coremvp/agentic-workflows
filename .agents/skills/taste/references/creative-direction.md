# Applying taste to creative direction

Read for a consequential writing, UI or demo direction. Use the current brief
and medium owner; Frontend alone owns the application's pictured discussion and
agreement contract.

## Visual and sequence decisions

Name the viewer's question, change in understanding, appropriate feeling and real
action/result that can carry it. Decide what remains legible and what the piece
must not imply. Cause/consequence, juxtaposition, accumulation and a useful
unresolved question are options, not a mandatory problem/solution formula.

Inventory actual assets and production tools before promising a render. Can we
capture the states/action, stage layers/masks/pointer/cuts/type/timing, inspect
playback and sound, and use the assets for this purpose? Mark the consequential
capability available/tested, available/untested, missing or proposed. Tool choice
follows the shot; this skill does not prescribe or install a renderer.

For a substantial direction, a compact annotated board names each exact source
and frame/sequence, observation, interpretation, proposed transfer and exception.
Inspect real pictures; inspect the sequence/audio for timing/cuts/sound. Use
original sketches or lawful source locators when reproduction is unavailable.
A verbal description is not an inspected picture.

Before expensive capture/animation, storyboard the consequential beats: rough
frame, viewer question, focal object, action/result, words, layers, transition,
approximate timing, asset source and proof limit. Timing comes from readability
and representative playback. In UI, attend to state continuity and next action
without importing decorative cinematic motion.

When work only announces the promise, diagnose the missing relationship: state
change, continuity, hierarchy, motivated cuts or time to read. More layers are
not automatically better; stillness can do the job. Repair the storyboard first.
If capture/compositing/playback is unavailable, return that production gap rather
than call another headline variation a finished demonstration.

Code inspects a representative difficult action/cut before extending the piece:
stills for hierarchy/actual-size reading, silent playback for sequence/rhythm,
sound separately for timing/meaning. Uninspected audio remains unreviewed. An
export alone proves neither motion nor sound. Label captured, seeded, reconstructed
and fictional material. Ask what an actual viewer understood before explaining
intent; without a response comprehension remains a hypothesis.

## Writing direction

Writing remains the production owner. Taste helps choose angle, form, voice,
rhythm, emotional register and revealing detail. Start from the person's state
and central supported claim; compare openings/structures only for a meaningful
choice. A preferred voice is not a factual claim or measured effect.

### Learn voice from actual passages

Read relevant founder-authored or explicitly accepted passages and any rejected
versions with the reason supplied. A brand adjective such as “bold” is not yet
an observable voice. Compare how the author opens, notices detail, makes claims,
uses technical language, moves between sentences, expresses uncertainty and ends.
Separate the author's words from an editor's contribution and current direction
from an old preference. Do not infer that acceptance of one fact endorses every
stylistic choice around it.

Turn a useful observation into three linked parts:

- Pattern: the specific choice visible in the passage, with its recoverable source.
- Boundary: the context where it helps and where it would become distracting,
  insensitive, misleading or unlike the author.
- Sample: a short original application to the current material, preserving truth.

For example, an inspected passage may earn directness by naming a failed action
before explaining the mechanism. That is more usable than “sound confident.” It
does not establish that every opening needs a failure or that the author wants
the same rhythm in an incident notice. A proposed sample is provisional until
accepted; do not call it the founder's established voice or audience-validated.

### Match tone to the stakes

Keep the recognizable voice while changing delivery for the situation. A playful
launch observation, a confused user's recovery and a consequential billing or
security explanation may need different degrees of humor, compression and detail.
Choose from the actual audience, stakes and accepted examples rather than impose
a fixed tone chart. In uncertainty or failure, clarity about what happened, what
is known and the next useful action may matter more than a clever line.

Expressive or humorous writing can keep factual boundaries. Distinctive voice
comes from what the author notices, selected detail, rhythm and honest uncertainty,
not borrowed catchphrases. Read aloud when spoken rhythm matters; text inspection
does not establish tested delivery. Ask whether the actual takeaway matches
intention and whether clarity erased something worth keeping. Keep meaningful
feedback through [studies](studies.md#learn-from-actual-response), with its context
and exceptions; never invent audience response to justify the sample.
