# Taste

Help someone notice, distinguish, choose and revise with a point of view. Taste
includes meaning, beauty, pleasure, humor, surprise and individual voice as well
as usefulness. Minimalism, familiarity and an easily verbalized rationale are
not universal measures of quality.

## Scope and owners

Start from the requested artifact or practice question, audience, intended effect,
constraints and main uncertainty. Scale attention to the choice; a small edit
may need a reread, an unfamiliar direction may need a study. Do not require a
mood board, full brief or review ceremony for every task.

Read project facts, brand and preferences from the current brief and existing
owners. A past liking is context, not a permanent law. Do not import another
project's palette, voice or admired references by default. Material questions
follow [Lead's](../lead-agent/SKILL.md) recommendation/why/tradeoff/one-decision rule.

[Marketing](../marketing/SKILL.md) owns audience, argument, proof, distribution
and learning; [Product](../product/SKILL.md) owns product truth;
[Writing](../writing/SKILL.md) owns prose, structure, voice and accuracy;
[SEO](../seo/SKILL.md) owns search intent and discoverability;
[Frontend](../frontend/SKILL.md) owns application UI and visual agreement.
Taste supplies judgment to the active job, not another design system, workflow
or permission to implement or publish.

For consequential writing, visual or sequence direction, read
[creative direction](references/creative-direction.md). For reference study,
critique or meaningful real feedback, including an approval, rejection or
revision, read [studies](references/studies.md). Apply it when relevant without
requiring a separate Taste request. A response need not yield a reusable lesson;
LGTM alone does not establish why a choice worked. Load neither support for a
routine factual correction. For voice, study actual authored/accepted and
rejected passages: observable pattern, contextual boundary and an original sample.
A proposed style remains provisional until accepted; adjust tone to the actual
stakes without turning one response into a permanent house voice.

Before a relevant consequential choice, search the headings/context in
[project judgment](references/project-judgment.md), then read only relevant
entries and their evidence. Explain which lesson informs the recommendation,
why it transfers and its exception, or that none exists. Current human direction,
Product truth and medium owners outrank old learning. This package ships no
observed project lessons; do not fill that gap with invented feedback.

## Separate the judgments

| Kind | What it establishes |
| --- | --- |
| Observation | What is actually present in an inspected artifact. |
| Interpretation | A plausible reading or predicted effect. |
| Preference | A person's response within its stated context. |
| Audience evidence | An actual observed response to a named artifact in a named context. |
| Craft | How a choice works within the medium. |
| Voice and originality | A distinctive way of noticing or expressing that belongs to this subject. |

Do not promote an interpretation into verified intent, simulated feedback into
audience response or technical correctness into aesthetic approval. Recognize
craft in work you would not imitate. An intuitive preference may remain
unexplained; do not manufacture a rationale.

## Observe, choose and revise

Use the loop where it helps, without iteration quotas or universal taste scores.
Experience the actual artifact before its reputation/commentary. Inspect images
before image-dependent claims, playback before timing/cuts and audio before sound
claims; unavailable evidence stays uninspected. In learning, invite a first
judgment before supplying yours.

Compare a meaningful alternative: difference, likely effect, where each belongs
and a credible exception. Transfer a mechanism into a small original study
rather than trace the surface. Critique intention against result; ask what someone
experienced before explaining yours. Practitioner critique, audience response
and your preference are different evidence. AI supplies readings, not human response.

Revise the consequential mismatch and inspect the result at actual size/pace.
Preserve character while improving clarity. When changes interact, compare
complete versions without pretending to isolate a cause. Revisit on unfamiliar
work or after distance when learning is the goal.

Keep substantial choices in the existing task artifact: intention/context,
alternatives/decision, reason or unresolved intuition, observation/evidence,
revision/result, scope/exception and what could change the judgment. Lead retains
only transferable consequential lessons through the feedback method; a mechanical
correction needs no learning record or new tracker.

## Evidence and production limits

A reference must teach a decision. Name the exact work/frame/timestamp, what you
inspected, relationship to adapt and where the analogy stops. Actual pictures
and annotations are more useful than adjectives. A source link is not proof its
pixels were inspected.

Separate still, motion, audio, runtime and audience evidence. A screenshot shows
composition, not easing, sound, usability, persistence or complete delivery.
A storyboard is a plan; a reconstruction is a study. State missing tools,
capture, assets, rights or craft early and the smallest needed next step. Do not
hide the gap behind repeated cosmetic variations.

Use original or appropriately licensed material. References grant no permission
to copy protected assets, install tools, access accounts, spend or publish.
Keep large research/third-party artifacts in the authorized task's scratch area,
not the skill. Deliver the requested artifact, consequential decision, evidence
actually inspected and remaining limits.
