# Agentic Workflows package

This repository owns reusable instructions, supporting references and thin client
adapters. Read README.md and the requested task before editing. Skills live in
`.agents/skills`; four engineering procedures live in `.agents/workflows`.
Keep portable content canonical; native adapters only configure discovery/actors.

Package work does not authorize an application feature, redesign or evaluation.
Preserve phase-only scope, Lead in the main conversation, Code production/checks
and independent read-only Review. Use a task record under ignored `tmp/` for
source bindings, changes and proof. Do not commit private feedback, secrets or
application data. Validate changed skills/formats/relative references and the
actual installation path. No new runtime/installer/CI without a concrete need.

A host application retains its own AGENTS/README/domain instructions; this file
is package-maintenance context and must not overwrite application instructions.

## Principle: great artists steal and own it

Great artists steal. We take the best ideas we find, make them ours, and own
what we build.

Study excellent work closely enough to understand why it succeeds. Take its
useful ideas, techniques and standards. Challenge their assumptions, combine
them with our judgment, and rebuild them around the actual problem we serve.
Every retained idea must earn its place. Cut what adds ceremony, simplify what
remains, and improve it through implementation, testing and real feedback.

Ownership means responsibility for the whole result: its purpose, decisions,
quality, tradeoffs, failures and maintenance. Understand and defend the method;
prove that it works in our context; revise it when evidence says otherwise.
An upstream reputation, copied catalog or renamed file cannot establish that
ownership. We must be able to explain why our version belongs and what makes
it useful.

This principle governs how we research, design, write and build. Express the
result as one coherent product organized around our users and existing
capability owners. Keep outside authors and source repositories out of the
skill architecture. Bring the strongest ideas into the work, then take full
responsibility for making the finished work excellent.
